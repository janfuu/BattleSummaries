==== Tough / party (5) ====
A Briarheart and six Forsworn wear the party down: the player is injured, Lydia nearly falls, and someone dies (Lydia in a duo, Aela in a party). Brelyna heals when she is there; alone, the player drinks potions.
  [remembered event]
    A battle was fought at the road to Whiterun. It lasted 34 seconds. Kaira, Lydia, Marcurio, Aela and Brelyna fought against Forsworn Briarheart and Forsworn x6. All 7 enemies were killed. Who killed the enemies: Kaira 2 (Forsworn Briarheart and Forsworn); Lydia 2 (Forsworn x2); Marcurio 1 (Forsworn); Aela 1 (Forsworn); Brelyna 1 (Forsworn). Most of the party's damage to the enemy was dealt by Kaira, Lydia and Marcurio. Lydia took the largest share of the enemy's blows. Brelyna kept the others on their feet with healing. Aela was killed by the fourth Forsworn. Kaira came out of it injured: a broken arm (dealt by the first Forsworn). Lydia was brought to the very edge of death by the second Forsworn, with almost no health left, and would most likely have died, but Marcurio killed the second Forsworn and Brelyna healed Lydia: Marcurio and Brelyna saved Lydia's life.

    Aela: took a brutal amount of damage; dealt only a small part of their side's damage; was killed by the fourth Forsworn; killed 1 of the other side.
    Brelyna: took solid wounds; dealt only a small part of their side's damage; was never near death; killed 1 of the other side.
  [Marcurio, about themself]
    Marcurio took solid wounds and dealt a fair share of their side's damage.
    Marcurio was never in serious danger.
    Marcurio killed 1 of the 7 who died on the other side: Forsworn.
    Marcurio saved Lydia's life. The second Forsworn, who had nearly killed Lydia, was then killed by Marcurio. While Lydia was at death's door, Brelyna healed Lydia with Healing Hands, restoring a good part of Lydia's health.
  [Aela, about themself]
    Aela took a brutal amount of damage and dealt only a small part of their side's damage.
    Aela was killed by the fourth Forsworn.
    Aela killed 1 of the 7 who died on the other side: Forsworn.
  [Brelyna, about themself]
    Brelyna took solid wounds and dealt only a small part of their side's damage.
    Brelyna was never in serious danger.
